**Intelligent system for automated business call analysis, helping companies improve customer service quality through AI-driven insights and comprehensive call evaluation.**

## 🎯 **What is Scanovich.ai?**

An AI-powered platform that automatically analyzes 100% of your business calls to:
- **🤖 Automate quality control** - No more manual call reviews
- **📈 Boost performance** - Data-driven coaching for your team  
- **💰 Increase revenue** - Identify missed opportunities and improve conversions
- **⚖️ Ensure compliance** - Automatic regulatory compliance monitoring
- **🔍 Extract insights** - Searchable transcripts and performance analytics

## 🏢 **Perfect For:**
- **Healthcare facilities** - Patient call quality and compliance
- **Call centers** - Quality assurance and operator performance
- **Sales teams** - Lead conversion and sales coaching
- **Financial services** - Regulatory compliance monitoring
- **Any business** with 50+ calls per month

## 🚀 **Key Benefits:**
- **95% cost reduction** in call analysis ($50K/month → $2.5K/month)
- **500-1000% ROI** in the first year
- **15-25% conversion improvement** through AI insights
- **30% reduction** in customer churn
- **Real-time analysis** - Results within minutes of call completion
