# Code of Conduct

This project aims to be useful, respectful, and safe for contributors, users, and maintainers.

## Our standard

Please:

- be respectful and constructive,
- focus on ideas and behavior, not personal attacks,
- assume good intent,
- keep technical disagreements calm and specific,
- respect privacy, security, and the sensitivity of real customer data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment or personal insults,
- discriminatory or hateful language,
- doxxing or sharing private information,
- posting real customer data, credentials, or sensitive production details,
- intentionally disruptive or bad-faith behavior.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to:

- GitHub issues,
- pull requests,
- discussions about the project,
- direct collaboration spaces connected to the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately:

- Email: `iamfuyoh@gmail.com`
- Telegram: [`@ScanovichAI`](https://t.me/ScanovichAI)

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or block participation when needed to protect the project and the people around it.

The goal is not bureaucracy. The goal is to keep the project humane, useful, and safe to collaborate on.
